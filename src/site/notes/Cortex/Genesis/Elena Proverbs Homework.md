### **Individual work Variant IV**

Below are some familiar proverbs and popular sayings.  
• Provide an equivalent - close or rough – which exists in the Romanian language. For the others, suggest a possible translation, literal or free.

1. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. – Frumusețea stă în ochii celui care o privește.
2. No news is good news. – Dacă nu vin știri noi, e de bine.
3. There’s no smoke without fire. – Până nu faci foc, nu iese fum. / De unde nu-i foc, nici fum nu iese.

8. Rome was not built in a day. – _Roma nu a fost construită într-o zi_
9. Every cloud has a silver lining. – _Fiecare nor are o margine argintie_
10. Small crisis teaches resilience. – _Criza mică învață reziliența_

• Make up a situation (280 words) in which to include at least three proverbs from the above exercises, as well as, at least 3 phraseological units.  

Maria was nervous about her first big presentation at university. On the way, the bus was late and her laptop battery warned low, so when she reached the hall she had only 30 minutes before her slot. She felt the clock like a weight on her chest, but she had practiced for days and believed that _practice makes perfect_. She heads to print the notes, only to notice a printer jam, which made her think she might _go out of the frying-pan, into the fire_. Without the notes in hand, she walked to the lecture hall, she noticed students chatting as _birds of a feather flock together_, everyone already forming small groups. 

She started anyway and _kept her eyes peeled_ for the teacher who could stop her for running over time. Halfway through a slide, the projector flickered, and she had to _bite her tongue_ instead of panicking. A student asked a sharp question but Maria stayed focused and kept her eyes on the prize. When a group member lent her a laptop cable, the moment felt like proof that _many hands make light work_, and they finished the setup fast enough to keep her talk on track. She smiled when a classmate nodded and grinned _like a Cheshire cat_, which calmed her more than she expected.  
  
Later, she met her group to plan the next project. They hit it off immediately, sharing different ideas and debating, and her friend Alia said, _a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush_, emphasizing that it was better to focus on what they could do now than worry about perfect outcomes. She knew progress took time, so she told herself _Rome was not built in a day_ and that small crisis teaches resilience. When she left, she felt tired but proud because even with little time, she learned to trust preparation, teamwork, and that _every cloud has a silver lining_.
