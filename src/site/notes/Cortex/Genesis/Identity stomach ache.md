# Stomach ache diagnostics 
**1. Pain location:**

- **Epigastric** → _Gastritis, peptic ulcer, pancreatitis, MI_
- **Periumbilical** → _Early appendicitis, small bowel obstruction_
- **Right upper quadrant (RUQ)** → _Gallbladder disease, hepatitis_
- **Left upper quadrant (LUQ)** → _Splenic infarct, gastritis_
- **Right lower quadrant (RLQ)** → _Appendicitis, Crohn’s, ovarian torsion_
- **Left lower quadrant (LLQ)** → _Diverticulitis, constipation_
- **Diffuse** → _Peritonitis, gastroenteritis, mesenteric ischemia_

**2. Timing relative to food:**

- **Pain worsens _with_ food** → _Gastric ulcer, gastritis, gallstones_
- **Pain relieved by food but recurs after 2–3 hrs** → _Duodenal ulcer_
- **Pain after fatty meals** → _Gallstones_
- **Pain when fasting (night pain)** → _Duodenal ulcer_
- **Pain unrelated to meals** → _IBS, ischemia, cancer_

**3. Pain character:**

- **Burning** → _GERD, ulcer_
- **Gnawing/dull** → _Gastritis, ulcer_
- **Sharp, localized** → _Appendicitis, peritonitis_
- **Cramping** → _Obstruction, IBS, gastroenteritis_
- **Radiating to back** → _Pancreatitis, aortic aneurysm, ulcer_

**4. Associated symptoms:**

- **Fever** → _Infection, inflammation (appendicitis, diverticulitis)_
- **Vomiting** → _Obstruction, gastritis, pancreatitis_
- **Weight loss** → _Cancer, malabsorption, chronic inflammation_
- **Bloody stool or melena** → _Ulcer, cancer, IBD_
- **Jaundice** → _Hepatitis, biliary obstruction_
- **Heartburn** → _GERD, hiatal hernia_
- **No bowel movement/gas** → _Obstruction_

**5. Physical exam clues:**

- **Guarding/rebound** → _Peritonitis_
- **Murphy's sign (pain on RUQ palpation with inspiration)** → _Cholecystitis_
- **McBurney’s point tenderness** → _Appendicitis_
- **Cullen’s or Grey-Turner’s sign (bruising)** → _Hemorrhagic pancreatitis_

**6. Diagnostics by condition:**

- **Ultrasound** → _Gallbladder, liver, kidneys, gynecologic_
- **CT scan** → _Appendicitis, diverticulitis, obstruction, mass_
- **Endoscopy** → _Ulcers, gastritis, tumors, H. pylori_
- **Blood tests**:
    - **CBC**: infection, bleeding
    - **Lipase/amylase**: pancreatitis
    - **LFTs, bilirubin**: hepatobiliary
    - **Lactate**: ischemia
- **Stool tests** → _Infection, blood, inflammation (calprotectin)_
