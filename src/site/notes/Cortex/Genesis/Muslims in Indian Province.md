# Muslims in Indian Province

- **Province Size (Population per Unit)**: 0.5M–1.5M
- **Estimated Province Count**:
    - **Min**: 215M ÷ 1.5M ≈ **143 Provinces**
    - **Max**: 215M ÷ 0.5M ≈ **430 Provinces**

| **Dār al-Hind al-Sharqī** _(Eastern Hind)_ | Bihar, West Bengal, Assam, Jharkhand                                               | 80–90             | Kishanganj, Murshidabad, Malda, Dhubri, Purnia     |

- **Dār al-Hind al-Gharbī** would be the **largest**, with dense Wilāyah populations across UP and central India. It would house major Islamic institutions, courts, and urban markets.
- **Dār al-Hind al-Sharqī** focuses on **high Muslim population density but weaker economic base**, ideal for Dawah, upliftment, and border diplomacy (e.g., Bangladesh).
- **Dār al-Dakkan** would integrate **high-literacy and tech-enabled regions** with strong legacy centers (Hyderabad, Kerala), forming a forward Dār for economic and scientific growth.

 India would contribute **~2–3%** of the global Dār structure. But due to India’s Muslim population (~11–12% of global Ummah), it would be **densely clustered and strategically crucial**, possibly forming a regional _Imarah zone_ (sub-Khilāfah sphere) if autonomy is achieved.
