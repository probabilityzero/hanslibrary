## **[[Waypoint/Domains/The Foundation/The Foundation\|The Foundation]]** 
This the umbrella organization for funding and that will incorporate:

- [[Cortex/Genesis/INDEX API\|INDEX API]]: (Integrated Database for Established Information) can serve as a comprehensive knowledge and publication aggregator, providing structured access to research papers, books, journals, reports, and other academic or non-academic written works.
	- Summariser 
	- Storage
- [[Cortex/Genesis/DID SSI\|DID SSI]]; Scholarly and other's User Management and Authentication Backend.
- **[[Portfolio/Projects/Scholarly\|Scholarly]]:** The central client app for INDEX—a dynamic, interactive, and universally accessible repository for research.
	- User libraries, connected notes, ai chats and so on.
- **[[Scholarly Community\|Scholarly Community]]:** A potential social network or collaborative space that connects folks, discussion, and facilitates data sharing.
- **[[Knowledge Central\|Knowledge Central]]:** A real-time, continuously updated encyclopedia that acts as a living, GitHub-like repository of scholarly insight.
