**WATER (PRIMARY ENTRYPOINT FOR PFAS & METALS)**

- Install an under-sink **RO + carbon filter system** (AquaPur, Ecosoft)
- Use **glass bottled spring water** or gravity filters (e.g., Berkey if imported)
- Never store or drink from **PET or soft plastic bottles**, especially sun-exposed
- Use **stainless steel or borosilicate glass bottles** for portable hydration

---

**COOKING & FOOD STORAGE**

- Eliminate non-stick/Teflon pans → Replace with **cast iron**, **ceramic**, or **stainless steel** (Vinod, IKEA, RockTawa)
- Ditch plastic cutting boards → Use **wooden** or **bamboo** boards
- Replace cling film/plastic wraps → Use **beeswax wraps** or **glass lids**
- Remove microwave usage for plastic containers → Switch to **ceramic/glass heating bowls**
- Avoid aluminum foil for high-heat → Use **parchment paper or banana leaves**
- Avoid canned foods with plastic linings → Prefer **jarred, frozen, or fresh** foods

---

**FOOD INPUTS (BIOACCUMULATIVE TOXIN LOAD)**

- Buy **seasonal/local produce** from farmer’s markets like _Piața Centrală_
- Soak/wash all fruits & vegetables in **baking soda + vinegar water** solution
- Use **organic grains/legumes** (NatureLand, Organic India if imported; local farm co-ops when possible)
- Replace refined oils with **cold-pressed oils**: sunflower, flax, pumpkin seed, ghee
- Avoid refined sugar → Use **dates, honey, jaggery, blackstrap molasses**
- Avoid commercial fast food/fried snacks → Replace with **homemade nutrient-dense meals**
- Store dry food in **airtight glass containers**, not plastic
- Avoid imported meats or processed deli meats (often treated with preservatives)
- Prefer wild-caught or local **river fish**, limit **tuna/swordfish (high mercury)**
- Avoid microwave popcorn, instant noodles, plastic-sealed heat packs

---

**PERSONAL CARE & COSMETICS** _(Endocrine disruptor & microplastic reduction)_

- Replace scented products → Use **fragrance-free or essential oil–based**
- Switch toothpaste to **fluoride-free brands** (Weleda, Logodent, Dabur)
- Replace aluminum deodorants → Use **alum stone, crystal stick, or baking soda-based**
- Shampoos & soaps: Avoid SLS/SLES, parabens, PEGs → Use **Sante, Biobaza, Dr. Konopka’s, Khadi**
- Face/skin creams: Avoid petrolatum, oxybenzone → Use **cold-pressed oils**, natural balms
- Lip care: No synthetic dyes → Use **beeswax-based or rose oil-based** balms
- Sunscreen: Avoid oxybenzone, avobenzone → Use **non-nano zinc oxide–based** brands
- Hair dyes: Avoid ammonia, PPD → Choose **herbal or henna-based dyes**

---

**CLOTHING & FABRICS (DERMAL LOAD & MICROFIBERS)**

- Avoid synthetic water-resistant or wrinkle-free fabrics → Prefer **organic cotton, hemp, or linen**
- Wash new clothes 2–3 times before use (removes formaldehyde residue)
- Use **guppyfriend bag** or microplastic filter in washing machine
- Avoid dry-cleaning unless specified **PFAS-free solvents** are used

---

**HOUSEHOLD PRODUCTS**

- Laundry: Replace with **Frosch, AlmaWin, Sodasan** (from DM stores)
- Dishwashing: Avoid triclosan, dyes → Use **Frosch Lemon, PureCult**
- Surface cleaner: Avoid bleach mixes → Use **white vinegar + essential oil** DIY or Ecover
- Air fresheners: Eliminate aerosols → Use **essential oil diffuser or beeswax candles**
- Insect repellent: Avoid DEET → Use **citronella, neem oil, or eucalyptus oil blends**

---

**AIR QUALITY MANAGEMENT**

- Install HEPA air purifier (Xiaomi, Coway) especially if in city
- Use activated carbon air fresheners or bamboo charcoal pouches
- Open windows during low-traffic hours (early AM or post-rain)
- Add **peace lily, snake plant, rubber plant** for VOC absorption
- Vacuum with HEPA filter; wet mop 2x/week

---

**SUPPLEMENTS FOR DETOX PATHWAY OPTIMIZATION**

- **Magnesium glycinate** (300–400mg): before bed
- **N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)** (600mg): with breakfast
- **Milk thistle extract**: 1 capsule or tea before meals
- **Omega-3 (DHA/EPA)**: 1000mg with lunch
- **Vitamin D3 + K2**: 2000–4000 IU in AM with fat
- **Zinc (15–30mg)**: after dinner
- **Probiotic** (10B CFU+): empty stomach or with fermented food
- **Glutathione (liposomal preferred)**: 250–500mg in morning
- **Selenium (1 Brazil nut/day)**: antioxidant enzyme cofactor
- **Curcumin + black pepper**: anti-inflammatory, taken with fat

---

**LIFESTYLE & BEHAVIORAL SHIFTS**

- Begin 15–20 min morning sunlight exposure (Vitamin D + circadian sync)
- Dry brush before shower → Stimulate lymphatic flow
- Use sauna/steam 2–3x/week if accessible → Mobilize stored toxins
- Add 20–30 mins daily movement (walking, stretching, yoga)
- Reduce blue light after sunset → Support melatonin detox cycle
- Avoid phone/laptop during meals → Enhance digestion & assimilation
- Eat largest meal midday → Align with liver + digestive rhythm
- Fast 12–14 hours overnight 3x/week → Stimulate autophagy
- Weekly castor oil packs on liver for drainage
- Meditate or breathwork 10 mins daily → Lower cortisol = faster toxin clearance
