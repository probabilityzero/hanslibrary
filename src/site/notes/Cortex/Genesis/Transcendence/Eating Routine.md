---

🌅 **6:00 am (upon waking):**

- Water (300–500 mL, warm or room temp)
- Electrolytes (if fasting/training early)
- Optional: Vitamin C (500–1000 mg)
- Optional: Greens powder if using
- **500 ml water + electrolytes** (or pinch of salt/lemon).
- Take **D3/K2, Omega-3, Creatine (5g)** _with this water_.
- Eat **1–2 almonds or 1 tsp ghee** to aid fat-soluble absorption.
- _Optional: prayer, stretching, sunlight exposure 10 min._

---

🌄 **7:00 am (breakfast):**

- High-protein meal (eggs, roti/oats, fruit, nuts).
- 30–40 g protein (eggs, Greek yogurt, protein shake, paneer)
- Oats/whole grains/fruit for slow carbs
- Nuts/seeds for healthy fats
- Berries/fruit for polyphenols
- Optional black coffee/green tea

- Fish oil (Omega-3) if not via diet
- Vitamin D3 (if additional needed)
- Multi-vitamin/mineral (if using)
- Creatine (3–5 g, can mix in shake)
- Take **Multivitamin + B-complex** with this meal.

---

🕙 **10:30 am (mid-morning snack):**

- Fruit (banana/berries) or
- Nuts + dark chocolate or
- Small protein shake (if needed for daily protein target)

- Probiotic capsule or fermented food if not at lunch
- Light intake: fruit, yogurt, nuts, or salad.
- _Optional green tea/black coffee if needed._

---

🌞 **12:30 pm (largest meal):**

- 40–50 g protein (lean meat, tofu, legumes, etc.)
- Vegetables (broccoli, leafy greens, crucifers)
- Healthy carbs (quinoa, sweet potato, brown rice)
- Healthy fats (olive oil, avocado, seeds)
- Fermented foods (kimchi, yogurt, etc.)

- **Calcimax water** (can take here instead of breakfast if preferred)
- Magnesium if split dosing (optional)
- Protein + complex carbs + vegetables + fats (dal/meat, rice/roti, veg, olive oil).
- Take **Zinc** with this meal.
- _5–10 min post-meal walk._

---

💪**3:30 pm (optional pre-workout):**

- Pre-workout: small carb (fruit/rice cake) + protein if needed
- If training later: dates + black coffee or fruit for energy.
- If training early, skip this.

---

💪 **5:30–6:00 pm (post-training):**

- Post-workout: 20–30 g protein (shake or meal) if not aligning with dinner
- Whey isolate + fruit or dates.
- _(Take Creatine here instead of morning if preferred.)_
- Creatine (if splitting dose)
- Electrolytes (if sweating heavily)

---

🌃 **7:30–8:00 pm (light dinner):**

- 20–30 g protein (light: fish, tofu, cottage cheese)
- Non-starchy vegetables (zucchini, spinach, salad)
- Small portion carbs if training day (quinoa, rice)
- Healthy fats (olive oil drizzle, nuts)

- Magnesium (200–300 mg if aiding sleep/recovery)
- Fish oil (if not taken earlier)
- Optional: Glycine or collagen for sleep support
- Optional (family time, social).
- Light protein + vegetables (milk + dates, paneer, veg soup).

---

🛏️ **12:00 pm (pre-bed):**

- Wind down, minimal light, deep breathing, optional journaling.
- Herbal tea or warm milk with turmeric (if desired)

- Magnesium (if not earlier)
- Glycine/collagen (optional for sleep)
- Ashwagandha (if not taken midday)

---

✅ **If waking at night (Elena/study) 1–4 am:**

– **Drink 200 ml water** immediately upon waking.  
– If _mildly hungry_:  
– 1 date or a piece of banana.  
– 5–7 soaked almonds.  
– _Or_ 1 tsp ghee + honey.  
– _If very hungry (late night study session)_:  
– Warm milk + pinch turmeric + black pepper + honey (calming, sleep-supportive post-study).  
– _Or_ a small protein shake if needed post-study.

---

**Avoid:**  
❌ Heavy meals at night.  
❌ High-sugar or junk food during late study.  
❌ Excessive caffeine; prefer hydration or herbal tea if needed.
