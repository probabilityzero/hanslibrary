# **A) COOKED GRAIN BOWLS**

### **1. Poha Bowl (your staple)**

**Ingredients (for ~600–800 kcal):**  
– Poha 1–1.5 cups (dry)  
– Peanuts 2 tbsp  
– Potato small ½  
– Ghee 1 tsp  
– Onion + peas optional  
**Calories:** 600–800  
**Why it works:** rapid carbs + mild fat + B-vitamins + great for morning cortisol rhythm.  
**Steps:** Rinse poha → sauté onion/peanut → add poha, peas, turmeric → finish with lemon/coriander.

### **2. Dalia Bowl**

**Ingredients:**  
– Dalia 1 cup cooked  
– Milk/curd ½ cup OR mixed veg  
– Nuts 1 tbsp  
– Ghee 1 tsp  
**Calories:** 550–750  
**Why:** slow carbs, keeps you full longer.  
**Steps:** Pressure cook dalia with water → mix with veg or milk.

### **3. Upma (semolina)**

**Ingredients:**  
– Rava ¾ cup  
– Mixed veg ½ cup  
– Peanuts/cashews 1 tbsp  
– Ghee 1 tsp  
**Calories:** 550–700  
**Why:** stable glucose, warm dish, great digestibility.  
**Steps:** Roast rava → sauté veg → add water → cook thick.

### **4. Sweet Potato + Legume Mix**

**Ingredients:**  
– Boiled sweet potato 200–250g  
– Chana/chickpeas ½ cup  
– Olive oil 1 tsp  
– Lemon  
**Calories:** 550–650  
**Why:** clean carbs + mineral rich + non-irritating.  
**Steps:** Boil → mix → season.

# **C) SMOOTHIE MEALS (FULL MEAL)**

### **8. Banana-Oats-Milk Smoothie (your recovery type)**

**Ingredients:**  
– Banana 1–2  
– Oats ¼–½ cup  
– Milk/curd 1–1.5 cup  
– Honey 1 tsp  
– Flax/chia 1 tbsp  
**Calories:** 600–900  
**Why:** fastest digesting breakfast, insane energy, covers entire micronutrient set.  
**Steps:** blend everything.

### **9. Green Smoothie (Blueprint-style)**

**Ingredients:**  
– Banana 1  
– Spinach 1 handful  
– Berries ½ cup  
– Nuts: 5–6  
– Seeds 1 tbsp  
– Water/milk  
**Calories:** 500–750  
**Why:** antioxidants + minerals + natural pump.  
**Steps:** blend.

### **10. Coffee-Protein-Oats Smoothie (no whey version)**

**Ingredients:**  
– Cold coffee  
– Oats ½ cup  
– Milk 1 cup  
– Peanut butter 1 tbsp  
**Calories:** 600–800  
**Why:** mental focus + morning strength.  
**Steps:** blend.
