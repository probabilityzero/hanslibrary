**I. The Central Universe — Canonical Continuum**

The _Central Universe_ is a structured mythohistorical and metaphysical narrative system, governed by an alternate temporal axis originating at **0 AH** (_Anno Harmoniae_), corresponding to 620 CE in the Gregorian calendar. All canonical events, characters, and cosmological developments adhere to a unified logic rooted in **archeological cosmology**, **ethical causality**, and **teleological design**.

**Core Structure**:

- **Chronotheology**: Time is interpreted as both historical and metaphysical progression, mapped across a divine eschatology. The AH calendar encodes epochs with ritual and theological importance.
- **Unified Mythos**: Myths, histories, and doctrines are absorbed, reframed, and made consonant with the metaphysical architecture of the Central Universe.
- **Prophetic Logic**: All events—past, present, or future—move toward _telos_, guided by prophecy, cyclic patterns, or divine harmonics.

**Temporal Layers**:

1. **Foundational Epochs** (pre-0 AH to ~300 AH):
    - Origin of divine covenants, rise of proto-prophets, mythic catastrophes (e.g., floods, celestial wars).
    - Recontextualization of ancient mythologies (e.g., Sumerian, Vedic, Mesoamerican) into metaphysical archetypes.
    - Archaeological resonances frame early metaphysical events in real-world ruins, artifacts, and oral traditions.
2. **Present Epoch** (~300 AH to 600 AH):
    - Earth-based regional narratives, filtered through the **New metaphysic**.
    - Religious reformations, ethical awakenings, spiritual conflicts.
    - Introduction of technological metaphysics—divine logic begins interfacing with matter and machine.
3. **Futurescape** (600 AH onward):
    - Interstellar diasporas of faith and culture.
    - Cosmic-scale moral struggles, theophanies, and apocalyptic revelations.
    - Culmination in _The Great Recurrence_, a convergence of all metaphysical arcs into eschaton.


- **Sub-Eras**: Organized by civilizational, prophetic, or cosmic events. Examples:
    - _The Flame Concord – 415–466 AH_: Unification of spiritual orders.
    - _First Stellar Expansion – 472–700 AH_: Birth of cosmic humanity.
- **Prophets and Orders**: Sacred figures guide history, often emerging across epochs; their teachings must align with the central ethical cosmology.
- **Artifacts and Relics**: Powerful objects encode divine law or eschatological knowledge—can exist across multiple time layers.

**Philosophical Boundaries**:

- **Metaphysical Monism**: All being stems from a singular metaphysical Source (_the New_); duality is apparent, not essential.
- **Mythical Reabsorption**: No external myth is canonical unless restructured within this framework—e.g., Gilgamesh is not a Mesopotamian king but an early "Covenant Breaker" or proto-prophet.
- **Ethical Absolutism**: While narrative nuance exists, the core cosmology enforces moral cause-and-effect via cosmic justice, karmic echoes, or divine punishment.

**II. Elseworld Cosmologies — Disjoint Realms**

_Disjoint Realms_ are narrative constructs that operate outside or parallel to the Central Universe, with distinct laws of metaphysics, time, and morality. They are not heretical to canon, but _non-compatible_, serving as experimental, mythopoetic, or genre-divergent terrains.

**Structural Taxonomy**:

- **Original Elseworlds**: Entirely new cosmological designs (e.g., post-entropy universes, dream-logic realities).
- **Inherited Elseworlds**: Derived from external cultural systems or mythologies, reimagined but not harmonized with the New.

**Function**:

- **Narrative Sandbox**: Allow thematic freedom (e.g., nihilism, moral relativism, atheistic worlds).
- **Genre Fluidity**: Support horror, surrealism, cyberpunk, weird fiction, or magical realism outside Central metaphysical constraints.
- **Contrast Tool**: Can be used to _highlight_ the philosophical or narrative choices of the Central Universe via juxtaposition.

**Integration & Interaction**:

- **Meta-Bridges**: Occasionally touched by "Observers," cosmic beings or tools that travel across boundaries to study or influence realms.
- **Non-Causal Links**: No event in Elseworlds affects Central Universe canon unless explicitly reabsorbed or retold from within.
- **Narrative Anchoring**: Some Elseworld stories are viewed _in-universe_ as parables, warnings, or visions seen by mystics.

**Cosmology Tags and Classification**:

- _World-Sundr 001_: A decaying universe where reality is slowly unraveling—used to explore entropy and resistance.
- _Mythgate Orbis_: A composite of Earth’s untouched mythologies—treated as historical what-ifs or potential fallen creations.
- _Eidolon Cluster_: Artificial dreamscapes with fluid identities, questioning the nature of personhood and soul.
