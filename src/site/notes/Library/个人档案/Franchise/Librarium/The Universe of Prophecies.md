**The Central Universe — Canonical Continuum**

The _Central Universe_ is a structured mythic-metaphysical system running on its own calendar (starting at 0 AH, aligned to 620 CE). Everything within it—events, characters, and cosmologies—follows a unified logic based on **archeological cosmology**, **moral cause-and-effect**, and **purpose-driven design**.

**Core Structure**:

- **Chronotheology**: Time unfolds as both sacred history and metaphysical development, moving toward a foretold end. Each age carries spiritual weight.
- **Unified Mythos**: Stories, doctrines, and histories from many cultures are reshaped to fit one metaphysical logic.
- **Prophetic Logic**: Events unfold by prophecy, cycles, or divine resonance—always pointing to a final goal (_telos_).

**Temporal Layers**:

1. **Foundational Epochs**:
    
    - Rise of divine laws, early prophets, and mythic cataclysms (floods, sky wars).
    - Ancient myths (e.g., Sumerian, Vedic, Mesoamerican) reinterpreted as spiritual archetypes.
    - Real-world ruins, relics, and oral legends provide _archeological validation_ for early metaphysical events.
2. **Present Epoch**:
    
    - Regional traditions evolve under a renewed metaphysical order.
    - Religious revolutions, moral awakenings, spiritual wars.
    - Technology becomes a medium for divine interaction—_techno-metaphysics_.
3. **Futurescape**:
    
    - Faith spreads across space; humanity becomes cosmic.
    - Moral and divine struggles scale up; revelations become apocalyptic.
    - All threads converge in _The Great Recurrence_, a return to the origin through completion.

- **Sub-Eras**: Defined by major spiritual, civilizational, or cosmic events. Examples:
    
    - _The Flame Concord_: Unity of sacred traditions.
    - _First Stellar Expansion_: Emergence of space-faring believers.
- **Prophets and Orders**: Sacred figures shape history, appearing across ages; their visions must align with core cosmology.
    
- **Artifacts and Relics**: Objects that hold divine law or prophetic memory—can exist in multiple timelines.
    
- **Archeological Points**: Real-world locations where spiritual memory is embedded (e.g., ziggurats, sacred mountains, submerged temples); function as proof-points and activation sites.
    

**Philosophical Boundaries**:

- **Metaphysical Monism**: All reality flows from one source—the _New_. Dualities like good/evil or spirit/matter are surface illusions.
- **Mythical Reabsorption**: External myths are _not_ canonical unless rewritten to fit the Central metaphysics. Gilgamesh, for instance, becomes a fallen prophet, not just a king.
- **Ethical Absolutism**: Moral actions always have spiritual consequences—through karmic return, divine balance, or cosmic judgment.
- **True Epistemologies**: Knowledge is valid only if it aligns with metaphysical law—intuition, ritual, and revealed truth rank above empirical doubt.

**Elseworld Cosmologies — Disjoint Realms**

_Disjoint Realms_ are narrative worlds outside the Central Universe. They follow different rules for reality, time, and morality. They are _not false_, just _incompatible_—used for experimenting with themes or genres the Central Universe doesn’t allow.

**Structural Types**:

- **Original Elseworlds**: Entirely new setups—like collapsing realities or dream-worlds.
- **Inherited Elseworlds**: Adapted from external myth systems (e.g., Norse, Aztec) but left outside canonical harmony.

**Purpose**:

- **Narrative Sandbox**: Test themes like moral chaos, nihilism, or spiritual absence.
- **Genre Play**: Support horror, surrealism, sci-fi, or magical realism outside core constraints.
- **Contrast Function**: Help clarify the Central Universe’s values by showing what it is _not_.

**Interaction Channels**:

- **Meta-Bridges**: Rare links via cosmic watchers, tools, or visions that cross realms.
- **Non-Causal**: Elseworld events do _not_ affect Central canon unless consciously retold within it.
- **Vision Framing**: Elseworld tales may appear in-universe as sacred dreams, mystic warnings, or metaphysical puzzles.

**Examples**:

- _World-Sundr 001_: A world unraveling into entropy—explores decay and defiance.
- _Mythgate Orbis_: An Earth that never lost its ancient gods—presents fallen or unchosen paths.
- _Eidolon Cluster_: Shape-shifting dreamworlds—questions identity, soul, and memory.
