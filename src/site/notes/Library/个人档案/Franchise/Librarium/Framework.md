**Library of Time and Space – Framework**

**1. IRL (Real World) – Damian’s Library**

- **Library of Time**:
    - Includes only factual, non-fiction, events.
    - Organized by chronology, location, or theme.
    - Acts as the real-world source for the Library of Time in INL.
    - Also includes 
- **Library of Space**:
    - Anchors fictional universes and stories.
    - Includes curated books—published or unpublished—that the author chooses.
    - Organized by universe, realm, or story arc.
    - Acts as the real-world source for the Realm of Stories in INL.

---

**2. IHL (Homeworld) – Mystical Library**

- **Library of Time (INL)**:
    - Mirrors real-world history and Homeworld events.
    - Must remain accurate; no deviations from real events.
    - Readers can experience historical events but cannot alter outcomes.
    - Serves as the foundation for all Librarium references.
- **Library of Space (INL)**:
    - Contains fictional universes, stories, and realms.
    - Universes can diverge from reality; internal logic must remain consistent.
    - Readers can enter and explore stories interactively.
    - Includes all curated stories acknowledged by Librarium as part of Realm of Stories.
    - Can include micro-stories to multi-book universes.

---

**3. Interaction Between IRL and IHL**

- The IRL library acts as the **anchor** and catalog.
- INL, these libraries allow **exploration** of those books and universes.
- Library of Time → real history → experienced in IHL as historical journeys.
- Library of Space → curated fiction → experienced in IHL as immersive universes.
- Both together form the **Library of Time and Space**, bridging real-world knowledge and fictional exploration.
