# [[Waypoint/Domains/House of El Han/HALO, Inc/HALO, Inc\|HALO, Inc]]


**1. Land & Sea Systems**  
– **Armory:** firearms, PGMs, artillery, railguns, siege engines, melee systems, personal/vehicle armor  
– **Naval Platforms:** warships, submarines, carriers, naval drones/underwater UUVs, naval missile & sonar systems  
– _Tools & Expertise:_ CAD/FEA, CNC/3D printing, marine composite fabrication, ballistic testing  
– _Competitors:_ [General Dynamics](https://www.gd.com), [BAE Systems](https://www.baesystems.com), [Huntington Ingalls](https://www.huntingtoningalls.com)

**2. Air & Space Systems**  
– **Aviation:** fighters, bombers, transport, surveillance/AWACS, helicopters, stealth UAV swarms  
– **Space Warfare:** military satellites (comms, ISR, navigation), anti-satellite weapons, orbital C2  
– _Tools & Expertise:_ composite layup, avionics/C4ISR integration, CFD, launch systems  
– _Competitors:_ [Lockheed Martin](https://www.lockheedmartin.com), [Northrop Grumman](https://www.northropgrumman.com), [SpaceX Starshield](https://www.spacex.com/starshield)

**3. Materials & Manufacturing**  
– Nanocarbon composites, plasma/ceramic armor, cryo-alloys, topology-optimized cores  
– Additive manufacturing, plasma spray, NDT, AI-driven QC in rapid prototyping  
– _Competitors:_ [DuPont](https://www.dupont.com), [Toray](https://www.toray.com), [Carbon 3D](https://www.carbon3d.com)

**4. Propulsion & Energy**  
– Pulse-detonation engines, railgun power supplies, hybrid-VTOL drives  
– Solid-state batteries, micro-reactors, DEWs (directed-energy weapons), EMP systems  
– _Competitors:_ [Raytheon Missiles & Defense](https://www.raytheonmissilesanddefense.com), [Rolls‑Royce Defense](https://www.rolls-royce.com), [Tesla Energy](https://www.tesla.com/energy)

– **Electronic Warfare:** RF/EMP jamming, radar deception, signal spoofing modules  
– _Tools & Expertise:_ Rust/Go payload frameworks, Metasploit, SDR labs, anechoic chambers  
– _Competitors:_ [NSO Group](https://www.nso-group.com), [L3Harris](https://www.l3harris.com), [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.com)

**6. Intelligence, Surveillance & Reconnaissance (ISR)**

**7. AI & Autonomous Systems**

– _Competitors:_ [Anduril](https://www.anduril.com), [Shield AI](https://shield.ai), [DeepMind](https://deepmind.com)

**8. Command & Control (C4ISR)**  
– Real-time battlefield communications, situational-awareness dashboards, tactical data links  
– Cyber–physical integration: SpecterGrid mesh networks, quantum keys, UX-driven control consoles  
– _Competitors:_ [Raytheon Integrated Defense](https://www.raytheon.com), [Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com), [Elbit Systems](https://www.elbitsystems.com)

**9. Support, Logistics & Services**  
– **Supply & Infrastructure:** base construction, transport convoys, mobile repair labs, field medical systems  
– **Private Operations:** contractor training, security services, covert ops support  
– _Competitors:_ [KBR](https://www.kbr.com), [Academi](https://academi.com), [DHL Defence](https://www.dhl.com)

**Proposed Projects & Learning Initiatives**

**Cyber & Electronic Warfare (Nexus)** – **-0 Minus O**: core autonomous C2 agent for network-scale infiltration; implement initial proof-of-concept in Rust + libp2p.  
– **SpecterGrid Lite**: skill-building mesh communication demo using Raspberry Pis and QUIC.  
– **Open-Source Fuzzing Tool**: develop LLM-guided fuzzer around AFL++ to discover simple vulnerabilities.

**AI & Autonomous Systems (Autonomy)** – **WarSeer Mini**: build a turn-based war-game simulator in Python with OpenAI Gym for reinforcement learning.  
– **Drone Sim**: use PX4 SITL and QGroundControl for autonomous flight scripts; no hardware needed.

**Intelligence, Surveillance & Reconnaissance (Recon)** – **ForgeLab Data Fusion**: prototype a data aggregator using Python, Pandas, and open satellite imagery APIs.  
– **OSINT Toolkit**: assemble and script tools (theHarvester, Maltego CE, Twint) for social-media intelligence.

**Support, Logistics & Services (Support)** – **Autonomous Convoy Planner**: simulate vehicle routing and scheduling using NetworkX and GIS data.  
– **Field Repair Robot**: design a low-cost Arduino-based robot arm for simple repair tasks in simulation.

**Land & Sea Systems (Armory)** – **Ballistics Calculator**: create a CLI tool in Go for projectile trajectory under varying conditions.  
– **3D-Printed Controller**: learn CAD and 3D printing by designing a simple rifle accessory or drone frame.

**Air & Space Systems (Flight)** – **Nano-Satellite Design**: use MATLAB/Simulink or Python-based tools to model CubeSat subsystems.  
– **UAV Autopilot Tuning**: deploy Ardupilot in SITL and adjust PID parameters for stable flight.

**Materials & Manufacturing** – **Composite Layup Demo**: learn basics via DIY carbon-fiber sheet application on simple molds.  
– **Topology Optimization**: design optimized structural parts.

**Propulsion & Energy** – **Solid-State Battery Cell**: study cell assembly from open protocols, simulate with Python thermals.  
– **DIY EM Launcher**: build a small coilgun prototype using salvaged electronics to understand railgun principles.

**Command & Control (Command)** – **Spectator Dashboard**: build a web dashboard in React for monitoring simulated battlefield metrics.  
– **Secure Chat**: implement an end-to-end encrypted chat using Signal Protocol libraries for practice.

**Cross-Divisional Learning** – **Capture The Flag (CTF) Challenges**: join platforms like Hack The Box, Pwnable.kr, OverTheWire.  
– **Open-Source Contribution**: contribute to projects like PX4, OpenPilot, Manticore, Intel PIN.  
– **Hackathons & Research Papers**: participate in IEEE competitions, read leading papers on arXiv, present mini-proposals.
