## **Now Entering: _Exodus_**

> An Operating System for Logistics and Transportation  

---

## **Exodus OS: Core Idea**

Exodus isn’t an OS in the traditional sense. It’s a **platform suite** made of interoperable apps/modules built for:

- Logistics coordination
- Transportation automation
- Energy optimization
- Freight finance
- Real-time intelligence across land, sea, air, and space

It’s the **“mission control + operating system”** for freight logistics and mobility infrastructure.
